The Poet's Philosophy

A masculinity spacious enough for tenderness

Adi Poetry writes toward a masculinity that can hold contradiction: desire without possession, strength without hardness, and vulnerability as a practice rather than a confession.

Here, love is not an escape from the self. It is one of the ways the self becomes more honest.

Love as strength. Vulnerability as practice. Masculinity lived with nuance.
